This Privacy Notice explains how 杭州桔瑞传媒有限公司 handles information accessed through the internal Jerry Studio Amazon Ads API application and its associated Login with Amazon integration.

1. Information we access

With the account holder's authorization, the application may access Amazon Advertising account identifiers, authorization information, advertising profiles, campaign and targeting settings, budgets, bids, reports, and advertising performance data such as impressions, clicks, cost, sales, orders, and attributed conversions.

The application does not intentionally collect payment card details, government identification numbers, passwords for third-party services, or consumer health information.

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate and maintain the authorized connection to Amazon Ads.
  • To retrieve and analyze advertising reports and account performance.
  • To manage advertising campaigns, budgets, bids, targets, and negative keywords.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and preserve an audit trail.
  • To comply with applicable legal and tax obligations.

3. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information or Amazon Ads account data. We do not share it with unrelated third parties except when required by law, necessary to protect our legal rights, or processed by a service provider acting on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

4. Storage and security

Authorization credentials are stored in access-controlled systems and are not intentionally exposed to unauthorized persons. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, contractual and recordkeeping requirements, dispute resolution, security, and applicable law. Data that is no longer required is deleted or de-identified.

6. Your choices and rights

The account holder may revoke the application's Amazon authorization at any time through Amazon's authorization controls. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with the application, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.
